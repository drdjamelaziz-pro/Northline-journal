100+ Common English Phrases for Daily Conversation

October 4, 2026

Learning a new language often feels overwhelming when focusing solely on complex grammar rules. However, real-life communication heavily relies on short, natural phrases that native speakers use every day.

Whether you are traveling, speaking with colleagues, or chatting with friends, mastering these practical expressions will make your spoken English sound much more natural and confident.

1. Greetings & Small Talk

First impressions matter. These simple phrases will help you start conversations smoothly in both formal and casual settings.

  • How’s it going? – How are you doing?
  • What have you been up to? – What have you been doing lately?
  • Long time no see! – Used when meeting someone after a long time
  • Can’t complain. – Things are going well
  • Same as usual. – Nothing new is happening
  • How’s life treating you? – How is everything going?
  • Nice to finally meet you. – Used when meeting someone for the first time
  • Take care! – A warm way to say goodbye

Using casual greetings sets a friendly tone and makes everyday interactions feel much more relaxed.

2. Asking for Help & Clarification

When you don't understand something or need assistance, knowing how to ask clearly is essential for smooth communication.

  • Could you give me a hand? – Can you help me?
  • Would you mind repeating that? – Please say that again
  • What do you mean by that? – Asking for clearer meaning
  • I didn’t quite catch that. – I didn't hear/understand what you said
  • Could you speak a bit slower? – Asking someone to slow down
  • Does that make sense? – Checking if the other person understands
  • Let me double-check. – Let me confirm to be sure
  • I'm not sure I follow. – I don't fully understand

Never hesitate to ask for clarification; using these polite phrases shows active listening and interest.

3. Expressing Opinions & Agreement

Sharing your thoughts and reacting to others is a key part of participating in discussions.

  • I completely agree. – Strong agreement
  • You’ve got a point. – Acknowledging a good argument
  • I’m not so sure about that. – Polite disagreement
  • That sounds great to me! – Showing enthusiasm for an idea
  • It depends on the situation. – Showing that there is no single answer
  • If you ask me... – In my opinion
  • I see where you're coming from. – I understand your point of view
  • Fair enough. – Accepting a reasonable explanation

Expressing agreement or polite disagreement helps keep dialogues constructive and respectful.

4. Daily Decisions & Expressing Preferences

From picking a restaurant to making weekend plans, these short expressions come in handy when making decisions.

  • It’s up to you. – You can decide
  • Whatever you prefer. – I am fine with your choice
  • I’m down for that! – I agree to join/do that
  • I'd rather pass this time. – Polite way to decline an invitation
  • Let’s play it by ear. – Let's decide as things happen without a fixed plan
  • Sounds like a plan! – Agreeing on a proposed plan
  • I’ll leave it to you. – Giving decision power to another person
  • Count me in! – I want to participate

Using natural decision-making phrases makes group interactions feel effortless and collaborative.

5. Apologies, Gratitude & Politeness

Polite language builds goodwill and resolves misunderstandings quickly in day-to-day encounters.

  • I really appreciate it. – A strong way to say thank you
  • Don’t mention it. – You're welcome
  • My bad! – A casual way to say "My mistake"
  • No worries at all. – It's fine / No problem
  • Sorry to bother you. – Polite interruption phrase
  • Thanks a million! – Thank you very much
  • It’s no big deal. – It is not a major issue
  • I owe you one. – I will return the favor in the future

Integrating these short expressions into your daily interactions will instantly elevate your fluency and confidence.
